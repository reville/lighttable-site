The whole image chain

Beyond a one-click look.

LightTable treats film simulation as the base render, not a filter pasted on at the end. Its controls follow the physical stages that make a photograph feel like film.

01

Capture

Start with scene-linear RAW, capture white balance, camera exposure, and film format.

02

Develop

Work with stock-specific response, development choices, diffusion, and physical grain.

03

Print & scan

Shape paper, filtration, preflash, glare, scan softness, and sharpening as distinct steps.

04

Grade

Finish with responsive tone, colour, detail, optics, crop, and export controls.
