Digital darkroom for macOS

LightTable Highly performant digital darkroom with realistic film stock effects.

  • Rust core with GPU-driven rendering on Metal.
  • Beautiful native macOS app.
  • Ultra-realistic film simulation, far beyond Lightroom, built on spektrafilm.
    • 23 film stocks with stock-specific spectral response
    • Physical grain scaled by stock and film format
    • Capture exposure, white balance, and development controls
    • Print paper, exposure, preflash, and Y/M filtration
    • Halation, diffusion, glare, scan softness, and sharpening
  • Easy migration from Lightroom, with XMP and legacy .lrtemplate preset compatibility.
